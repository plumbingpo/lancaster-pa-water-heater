How Reading Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which limits scalding risk and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on descaling every year if their water is hard.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing warning signs like rusty hot water, rumbling during heating, dampness under the tank, or hot water that runs out sooner than it used to, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one with water on the floor.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A tank that's just begun to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet the same sediment, ignored through a few more years, can overheat the bottom of the tank and shorten the life of the tank, turning a simple visit into a replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off. A quick call saves time, money, and cold showers.

What Goes Wrong With Local Water Heaters

In Reading, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tanks usually give trouble through thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
