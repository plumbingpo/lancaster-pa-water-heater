Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Lititz

Most early failures trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Flush the tank once a year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and test the relief valve each year so it doesn't seize. Set the thermostat close to 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. For a tankless heater in a hard-water area, plan on descaling once a year to protect the heat exchanger.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms such as rusty hot water, popping and rumbling, dampness under the tank, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one with water on the floor.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Take a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water begins corroding the tank itself, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a flooded utility room.

Hot Water Headaches Lititz Homeowners Face

Every type of water heater fails in its own way, and we work on all of them around Lititz. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through thermocouples and flame sensors, dirty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Tankless units show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
